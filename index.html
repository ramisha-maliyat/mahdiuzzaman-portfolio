About

I started in oceanography, went into maritime business for an MBA, then spent the last few years in the operational side of shipping — customs paperwork, cargo documentation, the parts of logistics that don't show up in a brochure but stop everything if they're wrong.

Alongside that I've done contract research on salinity-gradient power generation and marine spatial planning, and I keep one foot in photography and hospitality work on the side. I'm currently in Copenhagen for a master's in Environment & Development, looking at where supply chains and sustainability policy actually meet.
